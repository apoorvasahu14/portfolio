Augmented Reality GTM & 4-Tier Monetization Strategy

1. The Market Inefficiency

While AR filter usage exploded across Instagram and Snapchat, commercial production remained locked behind studios (AliveNow, Superfan Studio) charging $2,000–$20,000 per build - pricing out 99% of SMBs, creators, and campus events.

2. Primary Research & 5 Buyer Personas (N=60)

88.9% of surveyed buyers were willing to pay ₹400–₹3,200, and 59.3% preferred customizable templates over custom builds.

Brand Marketing • High-Ticket D2C CX

Experiential Retail & Consultative Sales Engine (Frootle India)

At Frootle’s HSR Layout experience center in Bangalore, shoppers researched ₹25K–₹80K+ appliances online (ECOVACS, De’Longhi, Tineco, XGIMI, DJI) but hesitated to buy due to setup anxiety. Built a repeatable 7-step journey yielding ₹20L+ direct revenue at a 45% close rate.

01. Low-pressure hospitality welcome
02. Category & lifestyle intent discovery
03. Home pain audit (layout, flooring, pets)
04. Transparent budget & EMI mapping
05. Curated shortlist (max 1–2 models)
06. Live sensory demo (espresso / vacuums)
07. Instant WhatsApp setup videos + same-day technician dispatch to cut return friction.
