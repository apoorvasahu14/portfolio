Product Marketing • GTM Case Study

Augmented Reality GTM & 4-Tier Monetization Strategy

1. The Market Inefficiency

While AR filter consumption surged on Instagram and Snapchat, commercial development remained locked behind enterprise studios (AliveNow, Superfan Studio) charging $2,000–$20,000 per project. This excluded 99% of small businesses, creators, and event organizers.

2. Primary Research & Persona Segmentation (N=60)

Conducted primary quantitative research revealing that 88.9% of potential buyers were willing to pay ₹400–₹3,200 for turnkey filters, and 59.3% preferred pre-made customizable templates over bespoke builds.

Independent Creators: Need high engagement to bypass algorithmic decline.
D2C Retail Brands: Virtual product try-ons to slash return rates.
Campus & Events: Low-cost virality for registrations and footfall.
Influencers: Sponsored interactive asset integrations.

3. Cost Architecture & 4-Tier Pricing Strategy

Modeled operational break-even (2 FTEs + SparkAR/Adobe tooling = ₹78,900/month baseline) and deployed a penetration pricing schedule with progressive margins:

Performance Marketing • Marketing Ops

Enterprise Lifecycle Email Engine & Agile Operations

1. The Scale & Results

Deployed 700+ promotional and lifecycle email campaigns in the financial services sector using Epsilon Marketing Suite and Adobe Workfront. Achieved a 24% open rate (outperforming the ~18% industry benchmark) and 2.5% CTR.

2. Diagnosing The 3 Waterfall Bottlenecks

  • Asset Retrieval Latency: Recurring/relaunched campaigns lacked searchable creative repositories, pushing proofs past deadlines.
  • Cross-Functional Review Friction: Communication silos between Client Marketing Managers (CMM), Channel Leads, and Operations led to launch delays.
  • Proofing Fatigue: Final legal creatives frequently spanned 10+ pages, inflating audit cycles.

3. The Agile Scrum Solution

Architected a streamlined campaign governance workflow based on the 3 C's (Card, Conversation, Confirmation):

• Daily Scrum Standup: Aligned Scrum Master, CMM, and Channel Leads on production specs.

• The "2-Call Maximum" Cap: Discrepancies were capped at 2 structured calls before executive resolution, killing weeks of async delay.

• Pilot QA Protocol: Pre-deployment testing on ~300-recipient cohorts within Epsilon to validate rendering and tracking before full blast.

High-Ticket D2C Experiential Marketing (Frootle India)

1. The High-Ticket Hurdle

Selling premium smart appliances (ECOVACS, De'Longhi, DJI, Tineco, XGIMI) priced at ₹25,000–₹80,000+ faces consumer inertia. Customers research online ("webrooming") but hesitate to check out due to perceived complexity and setup anxiety.

2. The 7-Step Consultative Conversion Funnel

1. Welcome & Hospitality: Low-pressure lounge environment in HSR Bangalore.
2. Category Intent: Discovering the primary lifestyle trigger.
3. Pain Audit: Evaluating square footage, domestic pet hair, and floor layout.
4. Budget Mapping: Structuring EMI / payment options.
5. Curated Match: Limiting options to 1–2 best-fit models to prevent decision paralysis.
6. Sensory Proof: Hands-on demo (operating vacuums, pulling espresso shots).
7. Onboarding Engine: Instant WhatsApp video setup guides + same-day technician dispatch.

3. Business Outcomes

Generated ₹20L+ in direct revenue with a 45% walk-in conversion rate, while eliminating return-rate friction through proactive onboarding.
